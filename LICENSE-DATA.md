## Scope

The licence covers the numerical observations, predictions, labels, recorded
analysis inputs and metadata in `data/controlled/` and `data/robustness/`, the
author-computed numerical summaries in `data/summaries/`, and the numerical
JSON/CSV results in `results/`, to the extent the authors are entitled to license
those contents. Executable program text embedded in records is software, not
numerical data; author-owned program text is covered by [LICENSE](LICENSE).

This licence does not cover third-party domain definitions, benchmark task
bodies, dependency code, model weights or rights in underlying third-party
inputs. The PDDL summaries were calculated by the project using domains from
LLMs-Planning; the domain bodies are not included. Third-party notices and
restrictions remain separate; see [sources and dependencies](docs/third_party.md).
